# The UI design compared with the backend

The design (`front-react-ui.png`, "Sedap - Modern Admin Dashboard") is a restaurant **admin** dashboard. This
page compares each of its parts with what the Spring Boot services offer, and says what Front-App does with it.
Rule: Front-App shows only what the platform really does; it never shows invented figures.

## Customer area

The design has no customer screens. The requirements' customer modules (Home, Restaurants, Search, Restaurant
Menu, Cart, Checkout, Orders, Order Tracking, Profile, Addresses, Offers) are built in the same visual language
(brand green, Plus Jakarta Sans, white cards), under `/` (the admin area lives under `/admin`): a top bar instead of the
admin sidebar, with the search box, "Restaurants" and the account.

| Module                          | Backend                                                                                                                                  | Front-App                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sign in, Register               | user-service `/auth/login`, `/auth/register` (password checked, no token yet)                                                            | **Sign in** and **Create an account** (17a); the customer is kept for the browser tab; an admin, owner or partner account is refused                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Home                            | catalog-service categories, restaurant-service list                                                                                      | **Home** (17a): "Find food" (what and where), the categories, a first look at the restaurants                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Restaurants                     | restaurant-service `GET /restaurants` (ACTIVE only, open now, name, city, cuisine)                                                       | **Restaurants** (17a): filters in the address (shareable), open or closed now                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Restaurant Menu                 | restaurant-service details and hours, catalog-service `/restaurants/{id}/menu`                                                           | **Restaurant page** (17a): address, phone, the week's hours (today marked, in the restaurant's time zone), the menu by category, "Veg only", search in the menu                                                                                                                                                            |
| Search                          | search-service: restaurants (typo-tolerant, cuisine facets), dishes (type, category, price), suggestions                                 | **Search** (17a) and the header's search box with suggestions (restaurants, dishes, categories)                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Cart, Checkout, Offers          | order-service cart and checkout, payment-service, promotion-service                                                                      | **Add** on the menu (one restaurant per cart; a new cart is offered), **Cart** (quantities, coupon, price, free-delivery hint), **Checkout** (address, payment method, note), **Payment** (card, UPI or wallet; cash on delivery needs none), **Offers** (applied to the cart) (17b)                                       |
| Addresses                       | user-service `/users/me/addresses` (at most 10, one default)                                                                             | **My addresses** and "New address" at checkout (17b)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Orders, Tracking, Profile       | order-service orders and cancel, delivery-service `/delivery/orders/{id}`, user-service profile and password, notification-service inbox | **My orders** (status filter), the **order page** (progress step by step, the delivery once the food is ready: partner, call while on the way, cash to have ready; history; **Cancel** with a reason before the restaurant accepts), **My profile** (name, phone, password), **Notifications** (the bell, mark read) (17c) |
| Photos, ratings, delivery times | **None of the services has them**                                                                                                        | Restaurants get a coloured tile with their initials; no ratings or delivery times are invented                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
