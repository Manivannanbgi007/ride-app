Ride App Booking Flow

How one order moves through the platform, from choosing dishes to a paid booking the restaurant has confirmed. Lanes show which part of the system acts; bands show the stage. Drawn from the services in this repo.

Read the flow as text
  1. Choose items. The customer browses or searches (search service), opens a restaurant menu and adds dishes. Catalog confirms each dish is on the menu and available. Cart rules: one restaurant at a time, up to 20 of a dish, up to 50 lines. The cart is priced with fresh prices, the coupon (re-checked by promotion), ₹40 delivery (free from ₹499) and 5% tax.
  2. Check out. The customer picks an address, a payment method and notes, then places the order with an idempotency key, so a retry never creates a second order. Order service checks the restaurant is open, every dish can still be ordered, the account is active and the address belongs to the customer. If anything fails, the customer sees why. Otherwise it reserves the coupon, saves the order with frozen prices, empties the cart and publishes OrderCreated. Cash on delivery goes straight to PLACED; online payment waits as PAYMENT_PENDING on the Payment page.
  3. Pay. Payment service checks the order is the caller's and still unpaid, takes the amount from the order (never from the browser) and charges through the simulated gateway or Razorpay. If the payment is declined, the order stays unpaid, the customer gets a push and can try again or cancel. If it is approved, SUCCESS is saved with a PaymentStatusChanged event. If the order is still unpaid it becomes PAID and PLACED; if the 15-minute timer already cancelled it, the payment is refunded automatically.
  4. Confirm. PLACED sends the customer an email and push, and the restaurant a push. If the restaurant accepts, the order is ACCEPTED, the customer gets "Order accepted" and can track it live. If it rejects, the order is cancelled with a reason and any payment is refunded. The customer may cancel while the order is unpaid or PLACED.

Order status

Set only by order-service, and only along these moves.

  1. PAYMENT_PENDING
  2. →
  3. PLACED
  4. →
  5. ACCEPTED
  6. →
  7. PREPARING
  8. →
  9. READY_FOR_PICKUP
  10. →
  11. OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
  12. →
  13. DELIVERED
  • CANCELLEDby the customer while PAYMENT_PENDING or PLACED, by the 15-minute timer while PAYMENT_PENDING, or by an admin until pickup
  • REJECTEDby the restaurant while PLACED

Cash on delivery orders skip PAYMENT_PENDING and start as PLACED. Dashed statuses come after the booking is confirmed (kitchen and delivery).

Payment status

Owned by payment-service. order-service keeps its own copy: PENDING, PAID, FAILED or REFUNDED.

  1. INITIATED
  2. →
  3. PROCESSING
  4. →
  5. SUCCESS
  6. →
  7. REFUNDED
  • FAILEDfrom INITIATED or PROCESSING; the customer can start a new attempt

With Razorpay, a failed try keeps the payment PROCESSING so the customer retries on the same Razorpay order. A partial refund keeps SUCCESS and records the refunded amount.
